Track detail

📡 Auto-DRS

Inside a DRS zone and within 1.0 s of the car ahead, your rear flap opens by itself for extra top speed. It closes when you brake.

⚡ ERS boost

Braking recharges the battery. Hold Space on a straight for a blue-trail power boost.

🔧 Pit stops

Drive into the pit lane on the right of the start straight, stop in your team's colour-marked box and wait 3 s: new tyres and a repaired wing. AI cars pit when their tyres wear out. Steering and braking assists (Off/Low/High) and the racing line (L) are in Settings.

🛞 Tyre grip

● Soft grips most but wears fast. ● Medium balances. ● Hard lasts. Worn tyres lose cornering grip.
